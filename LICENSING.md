**GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later**
(`AGPL-3.0-or-later`). The complete license text is in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).

The AGPL is an open-source license and permits commercial use. Anyone using,
modifying, distributing, or operating a network-accessible modified version
must comply with the AGPL's applicable terms, including its corresponding
source obligations.

## Alternative commercial licensing

Organizations that want to use LMC-5 under proprietary terms, embed it in a
closed-source product, or operate a modified network service without complying
with the AGPL may request a separate written commercial license from the
copyright holders.

See [`COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md`](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md). That document describes
the commercial-licensing path but does not itself grant commercial rights.

## Versions through 0.2.x

LMC-5 releases through and including tag `v0.2.0` were published under the MIT
License. Those grants remain valid and are not revoked by the license change.

The new license applies to version 0.3.0 and later. Receiving an older MIT
release does not grant MIT rights to later AGPL-covered changes.

## Third-party material

Third-party dependencies and separately identified third-party material remain
under their own licenses. LMC-5's license does not replace or narrow those
licenses.

Because the project may offer alternative commercial licenses, third-party
